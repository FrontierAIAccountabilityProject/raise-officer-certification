# RAISE Officer Certification

A public-domain draft amendment to Article 44-B of the New York General Business Law requiring named senior officers of large frontier developers to certify compliance—or acknowledge noncompliance—with existing frontier-AI transparency and reporting duties.

The proposal builds on the RAISE Act, Chapter 96 of the Laws of 2026, and New York’s existing executive-certification structure under 23 NYCRR § 500.17(b).

Its purpose is narrow: place natural persons’ names on duties New York has already enacted. It does not expand the models or developers covered by the RAISE Act, create a new regulator or safety standard, authorize private lawsuits, or create a new criminal offense.

**Status:** Initial research draft. Not enacted law.
