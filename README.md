# RAISE Officer Certification

A public-domain research draft amending Article 44-B of the New York General Business Law to require named natural persons at large frontier developers to certify compliance, or publicly acknowledge noncompliance, with duties New York has already enacted.

The proposal builds on the RAISE Act, Chapter 96 of the Laws of 2026. It adds a proposed § 1422-a requiring:

- certification before or concurrently with a new or substantially modified frontier-model deployment;
- a prospective certification for covered models already deployed or in extensive internal use when the amendment takes effect;
- an annual compliance certification or acknowledgment of noncompliance;
- personal signatures from the developer's highest executive, the highest executive of any ultimately controlling affiliate, the senior officer responsible for the frontier AI framework, and any additional natural person with final material decision authority over deployment or extensive internal use;
- reasonable inquiry and reporting systems sufficient to prevent retained authority from being hidden behind ignorance, delegation, committees, affiliates, contractors, or artificial intelligence systems;
- a searchable public register identifying the developer, model, filing, and required signers while allowing lawful redaction of protected technical information;
- civil enforcement against a required signer for failure to exercise due care in performing that signer's own duties; and
- personal payment of individual civil penalties, without corporate indemnification, insurance, reimbursement, or compensation gross-ups.

The draft does **not** change the RAISE Act's model threshold or revenue threshold, regulate ordinary AI systems, create a new regulator or technical safety standard, authorize a private right of action, or create a new criminal offense. The Penal Law § 210.45 notice uses New York's existing false-written-statement mechanism.
